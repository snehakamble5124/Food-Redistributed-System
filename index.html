How It Works

From surplus to serving in four simple steps

1

List Donation

Donors add food details, quantity, expiry, and location with a single form.

2

Smart Matching

Our distributed system matches donations to NGOs based on proximity, needs, and priority.

3

Reserve & Pickup

NGOs accept donations using mutual exclusion. Volunteers are assigned for pickup.

4

Deliver & Track

Real-time delivery tracking with WebSocket updates until food reaches its destination.
